My Fire Island Journey

My recent escape to Fire Island was nothing short of magical. From walking past the historic, towering lighthouse to exploring the sun-bleached boardwalks of the Sunken Forest, every moment felt like a step away from reality. The highlights of the trip included catching a breathtaking Atlantic sunset, spotting local white-tailed deer hiding in the thickets, and enjoying miles of untouched, peaceful sand dunes away from the city noise.
